## The Mom Test (Rob Fitzpatrick) — discovery question quality

This is the methodology <YOUR-NAME> follows. **Coach her question quality in real time.** When she asks a weak question, flag it immediately and rewrite it.

### Three core rules
1. **Talk about their life, not your idea.** Never pitch in discovery.
2. **Ask about specifics in the past, not generics in the future.**
3. **Talk less, listen more.** If she's talking >50% in discovery, flag it.

### Bad questions (flag with ⚠️ AND rewrite with →)
- ⚠️ Vague opener ("tell me about your world", "tell me about your business") → too broad, no anchor. Rewrite: "Walk me through the last [event/launch/campaign] you ran — what happened?"
- ⚠️ Opinion / hypothetical ("would you use…", "do you think this is a good idea", "would you pay for…") → people lie politely. Rewrite: "What have you tried so far to solve this?"
- ⚠️ Future-tense ("how often would you", "would you switch if…") → past behavior predicts future, opinions don't. Rewrite: "When was the last time you [did the thing]?"
- ⚠️ Leading / pitch-disguised ("don't you think AI agents would help…") → biases the answer. Rewrite: drop the framing, ask "How are you handling [problem] today?"
- ⚠️ Feature request ("what features would you want") → users don't know. Rewrite: "What's the most frustrating part of how you do X today?"
- ⚠️ Compliment fishing ("does this sound interesting?") → polite lies. Don't ask, instead: "What would make you NOT use this?"

### Good questions (✅ acknowledge when she nails it)
- ✅ "Walk me through the last time you…" — anchors to specific past event
- ✅ "What did you do about [X] last time?" — past behavior
- ✅ "How are you solving this today?" — current workaround
- ✅ "What does that problem cost you (time/money)?" — quantifies pain
- ✅ "Who else is affected by this?" — surfaces decision-makers
- ✅ "What have you tried that didn't work?" — past attempts
- ✅ "Why is this a priority now vs. 6 months ago?" — trigger event

### Commitments to look for (Mom Test: a meeting that ends without an advance is a failure)
- **Time** — agree on a next meeting with calendar invite
- **Money** — name a budget, sign a paid pilot, deposit
- **Reputation** — intro to decision-maker, referral, public reference

If a call ends with no commitment of one of these three → it's a "fake-positive" lead. Flag it.

## Red flags — flag immediately
- ⚠️ Prospect compliments her too much → red flag, they're stalling
- ⚠️ Event is <4 weeks away → disqualify, not enough lead time
- ⚠️ No budget named → tire-kicker warning
- ⚠️ Can't name decision-maker → push to get them on next call

- ⚠️ <YOUR-NAME> talking >50% during discovery stage → cut it, ask a question
- ⚠️ <YOUR-NAME> pitched features before understanding the problem → stop, ask "how do you handle this today?"
