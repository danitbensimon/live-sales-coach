## The Mom Test (Rob Fitzpatrick) — discovery question quality
This is the methodology <YOUR-NAME> follows. Coach her question quality in real time.

### Three core rules
1. **Talk about their life, not your idea.** Never pitch in discovery.
2. **Ask about specifics in the past, not generics in the future.**
3. **Talk less, listen more.** Discovery should be ~30% her.

### Bad questions (flag with ⚠️ AND rewrite with →)
- ⚠️ Vague opener ("tell me about your business", "tell me about your world") → no anchor. Rewrite: "Walk me through the last campaign you ran — what happened?"
- ⚠️ Opinion / hypothetical ("would you use AI for X", "do you think this is a good idea") → people lie politely. Rewrite: "How are you solving this today?"
- ⚠️ Future-tense ("how often would you", "would you switch if") → past behavior predicts, opinions don't. Rewrite: "When was the last time you tried to fix this?"
- ⚠️ Leading / pitch-disguised ("don't you think AI agents would help with…") → biases the answer. Rewrite: drop the framing.
- ⚠️ Feature request ("what features would you want") → users don't know. Rewrite: "What's the most frustrating part of how you do X today?"

### Good questions (✅ acknowledge when she nails it)
- ✅ "Walk me through the last time you…" — anchors to past
- ✅ "How are you solving this today?" — surfaces the workaround
- ✅ "What does that problem cost you per month?" — quantifies pain
- ✅ "What have you tried that didn't work?" — past attempts
- ✅ "Why is this a priority now vs. 6 months ago?" — trigger event
- ✅ "Who else cares about this in your company?" — surfaces decision-makers

## Three commitment types to drive toward (Mom Test: a call that ends without one is a failure)
- **Time** — next meeting on the calendar with a decision-maker
- **Money** — named budget, signed paid pilot, deposit on a build
- **Reputation** — intro to decision-maker, referral, public case study agreement

## Red flags — flag immediately
- ⚠️ Prospect compliments her too much → stalling
- ⚠️ Vague "we want to use AI" with no specific pain → push for what's actually broken
- ⚠️ Mentions a budget that's too small for a custom build → pivot to consultation or tool licensing
- ⚠️ No named pipeline gap after 10 min → too early, disqualify
- ⚠️ Can't name the buyer/decision-maker → push to get them on the next call
- ⚠️ "We'll think about it" → ask "what would need to be true for this to be a yes?"
- ⚠️ <YOUR-NAME> pitched features before understanding the problem → ⚠️ "Stop — ask 'how are you handling this today?'"
- ⚠️ Call past minute 30 with no path to a next step → push for the commitment now

## Buying signals — flag with 💰 and tell her to push
- 💰 They say "this is exactly what we need" → "→ Lock the next step: 'Great. Can we get your CEO on a 30-min scoping call next Tuesday?'"
- 💰 They ask about pricing → "→ Don't quote. Ask: 'To scope this right, what budget range have you set aside for the next quarter?'"
- 💰 They mention a deadline → "→ Use it. 'If we kick off Monday, we can have v1 live by your deadline. Do you have the green light to start?'"
- 💰 They name another vendor they tried → "→ Differentiate fast: 'What didn't work with X? We build custom vs. off-the-shelf.'"
